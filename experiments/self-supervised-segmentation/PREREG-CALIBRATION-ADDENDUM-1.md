## The diagnosis (a hypothesis, stated before it is tested)
The bar compares a candidate's mean tagger-margin change over ALL tokens of the changed sentences with random same-length cuts. The new PIECES themselves carry the change: a lone capital letter is a confidently tagged token, so cutting one off raises the sentence's mean confidence on any text, and the null (random cuts of random words, mostly lowercase pieces) is not matched on what the piece is. So the signal is confounded by piece identity, not noise in the statistical sense. The codec session's effect measure avoids it by scoring only the tokens OTHER than the stem and the clitic.
## The change for run 2 (CTX mode)
The change of a cut is measured on the CONTEXT tokens only: for every sentence, the tokens that were not cut and are therefore in both versions; the new pieces and the original cut token are excluded. A sentence with no context token is skipped. The same measure is used for the candidate and for the null draws. Condition C plants `zq` on eight tokens of batch 1 (not three) so it is evaluable.
