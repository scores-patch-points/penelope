Correction, made before any usable result was seen: M = max(programs held + 1, P), where P is the patience at which a run of failures stops being distinguishable from noise at even odds, derived from the null itself: each evaluation under pure noise has probability 1/(draws + 1) of beating the null's maximum, so P = ceil(ln 0.5 / ln(1 - 1/(draws + 1))); with 30 null draws P = 22. The 30 draws and the even odds are the only numbers in it, and both were already in the protocol or are the median by definition.
