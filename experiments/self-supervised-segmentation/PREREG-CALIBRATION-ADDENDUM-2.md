# ADDENDUM 2 to PREREG-CALIBRATION.md; written 2026-11-20 after run 2 (context-only, calibration-result-ctx.json), BEFORE the power test
Run 2 showed the context-only measure calibrated on noise (4.0 to 5.9% good per batch, 0.89% replicated) but recovering only 1 of 5 planted function words at 40 sampled sentences (PB3 falsified). Two explanations: (i) BLIND: a local repair lives in the cut token's own pieces, which the measure excludes, so it cannot see it at any sample size; (ii) UNDERPOWERED: the effect on neighbours is small (clitic effects were +0.13 to +0.94 against null maxima of 0.06 to 0.38) and 40 sentences cannot resolve it.
## The power test (POWER=1, CTX=1, SAMPLE=200)
Condition B: only the five derived planted words are evaluated, per batch, with 200 sampled sentences (null: 30 draws of 200). Condition A: 150 random candidates per batch (seeded) are evaluated with 200 sampled sentences, to see whether the noise rate stays calibrated at the larger sample.
PP1 (underpowered, not blind): at least four of the five planted words are good in at least two of the four batches. FP1: fewer than three (then the context-only measure is blind to local repairs and cannot be the colony's only signal). PP2 (calibration holds): the noise good-rate per batch stays at most 2/31 (6.5%). FP2: above 2/31.
Between three and four planted words is reported as inconclusive. No number is tuned afterward.
