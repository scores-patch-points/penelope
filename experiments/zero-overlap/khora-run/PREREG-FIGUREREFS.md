# PREREG-FIGUREREFS: referents by FIGURE POSITION, not by proper name (filed before it was run). EXPLORATORY, English.
User: "it shouldn't only be about proper name things; the thing can just be the figure position." So a referent is whatever occupies the figure position of a relation: a noun phrase, abstract or concrete, named or not. Identity is positional and within a document: two relations whose figure ends have the same head noun in the same source are the same referent (alias resolution by head, the same job the cast does for names).
Material: the 3,590 English clauses of the lexical-analysis clause run (five registers = five documents), concatenated per register as paragraphs; khora's host reader (sessionRelations) gives SVO relations with offsets, mapped back to clauses.
Figure position (declared, mechanical, from phasepost.js's own grain rule): for a relation {end1, label, end2}: if the label is a copula form, the figure is end1 (what is being characterised); otherwise the figure is end2 when it is present and not locative-led (not starting with a locative preposition), else end1. A figure end that is a pronoun, a bare determiner or a single character is dropped (typed, counted). Head noun = the last word of the end after removing determiners and prepositions, lowercased; no lemmatiser.
Referent = (register, head). Entities for kind induction: referents that figure in >= 4 relations in their register.
Evidence for kinds (about the referent's behaviour, not its string): share of its appearances as figure by copula vs non-copula; share of appearances in end1 position vs end2 position; dominant predicate class acting on it (copula / locative preposition / other preposition / verb); grain mix from phasepost.js::grainOf (Figure / Pattern / Ground, dominant); number of distinct clauses (tercile; reported as salience, kept in because here it is a legitimate property of a figure); NOT fed in: the head string, the register, Claude's labels.
Kinds: khora's inducer (kernel/entity-kind-induction.js), its own null and `passed`. Control: each evidence column shuffled independently (seed 20261118).
After the kinds: each kind's size, parameters and example heads; and the test that matters: for every referent take the MAJORITY value of Claude's Q3 ("what kind of thing is acted on": BACKGROUND / PARTICULAR / PATTERN) and Q2 ("level of reality": EXISTENCE / ORGANIZATION / MEANING) over the clauses in which it is the figure; NMI between kind membership and each, against a permutation null (1,000 shuffles, seed 20261118; above chance = above the 95th percentile). Also NMI with the register (topic control).
Falsifier (in advance): fewer than 3 kinds pass, or the control finds as many, or NMI with Q3 is within the permutation null (the figure referents' kinds do not track "what kind of thing is acted on"). Coverage (share of clauses with a figure referent) is reported, not a falsifier.
Not claimed: that head-noun identity is the right identity (it conflates "the state" of different things; no coref); anything about other languages or held-out.

## ADDENDUM-1 (user: refine it; filed before the refined run; first-run result seen: 48 entities, control = real). Declared refinements, all changes after seeing results, so this is the second look at the same material:
(1) NOUN FILTER: a head must appear in the UniMorph English lexicon's NOUN or NOUN|VERB class (priors-en/pos-eng-lexicon.json; giver UniMorph English); heads absent from both are dropped and counted. Known weakness stated now: the lexicon lists some function words as nouns ("if", "due"), and a noun-verb ambiguity is accepted.
(2) LEMMA MERGE: a head ending in -ies maps to -y, ending in -es or -s maps to the stem, when the stem is in the same noun lexicon; no other morphology.
(3) RECURRENCE FLOOR: referents figuring in >= 2 relations (was >= 4).
Unchanged: figure-position rule, the evidence columns, the inducer and its own null, the independent-column control, the NMI tests against Claude's Q3 and Q2 majority labels and against register, and the falsifier (fewer than 3 kinds pass; control finds as many; NMI with Q3 within the permutation null). Added for this iteration, written now: a kind counts as informative about "what kind of thing is acted on" only if NMI(kinds, Q3) is above its null95 AND the control finds strictly fewer kinds than the real run.
