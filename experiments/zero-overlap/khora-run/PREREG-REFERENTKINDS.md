# PREREG-REFERENTKINDS: kinds of referent, and kinds of relationship between referents (filed before it was run). EXPLORATORY, English.
User correction: "these are about referents not strings". Entities here are REFERENTS (khora's cast: things resolved across their surface forms, pronouns and aliases), and a relationship is a link between two resolved referents. Strings are used ONLY to resolve an end of a relation to its referent (alias resolution), never as a feature.
Material: the 321 English Wikipedia leads (leads-en-div.txt), khora's constitutional host reader (createSession / admitChunked / sessionReferents / sessionRelations), no coref prior (unprimed, as in the earlier runs).
Entity = a referent with >= 2 mentions. Evidence (all referent-level, bucketed by corpus terciles where numeric, tercile edges declared by the data, no tuned cut): individuation (as khora labels it); agency; coupling; mentions; frames; naming-sentence share; frame span (lastFrame - firstFrame); relation count; out-share (share of its resolved relations in which it is the first end); dominant predicate class of its relations (copula / locative preposition / other preposition / verb / none: a closed-class label on the relation, not a lexical feature of the referent). No display name, no surface form, no article category.
Kinds of referent: khora's kind inducer (kernel/entity-kind-induction.js::induceEntityKindCandidates), its own null and `passed` flag.
Kinds of relationship: a relation (A, predicate class, B) whose two ends resolve to referents that both belong to induced kinds is typed by (kind(A), predicate class, kind(B)); I report the most frequent types with examples (display names for reading only).
Control (declared, correctly this time): each evidence column shuffled independently across referents (seed 20261118); the inducer should find fewer kinds.
Then, descriptively: a kind's relation to the three questions is read from its members' relations classified by phasepost.js with ActPrior@1 (operator and grain where attested); no claim beyond a table.
Falsifier (in advance): fewer than 3 referent kinds pass the inducer's own null; or the control finds as many; or every kind is defined by the same single dimension (e.g. only mention count).
Not claimed: that these are the cube's or Aristotle's kinds; other languages; held-out.
