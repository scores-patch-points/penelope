The relationships the binding layer finds (1,470 directed co-arrival links) are not, here, typed by the kinds of their ends.
Limits: draws 100 (p-value floor 0.0099) and overlap >= 3 were my declared bounds; the register of arrivals is my stand-in, not khora's carried entity state; coref unprimed (pronouns and descriptors do not resolve); 57% of candidates pass all three nulls, which suggests the nulls are permissive on short, repetitive, same-article co-arrival; encyclopedic leads are heavy on is-a and located-in; one corpus, English.
What this does NOT show: that kinds of relationship do not exist. It shows these instruments (co-arrival links, bucketed referent profiles, khora's inducer) do not find them on this material.
