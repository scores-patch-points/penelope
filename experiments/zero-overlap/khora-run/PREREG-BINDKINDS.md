# PREREG-BINDKINDS: kinds of referent from their BOUND neighbours, and kinds of relationship as pairs of kinds (filed before it was run). EXPLORATORY, English.
Follows PREREG-REFERENTKINDS (referent kinds were mostly a prominence gradient; only 4% of relations resolved at both ends). User: yes, binding links.
Relations: khora's binding layer (emergence/binding.js::readLinks), between REFERENTS (khora cast referents with >= 2 arrival sentences; an arrival is a sentence containing one of the referent's alias-resolved surfaces; stand-in register, disclosed). Declared parameters: window 1, draws 500, seed 20261118, totalUnits = the sentence count; candidate pairs prefiltered to overlap >= 2 co-arrivals (declared to bound the run); a link is SIGNIFICANT iff displacement, reversal and reseed nulls are all p < 0.05, and DIRECTED iff the layer set a direction. Only significant directed links are used as relations.
Referent-level evidence for kind induction (no strings, no prominence): individuation, agency, coupling (khora cast fields); bound degree tercile; out-share tercile (share of its links where it is the earlier end a->b); mean link strength tercile; number of distinct articles its bound neighbours arrive in, tercile. Mentions, frames, span and naming share are HELD OUT of the evidence (reported after, to see whether kinds still track prominence).
Two passes: pass 1 induces kinds from the evidence above; pass 2 adds, per referent, one evidence entry per bound neighbour: bindsTo = the neighbour's pass-1 kind (or none). Final kinds = the pass-2 induction.
Kinds: khora's inducer (kernel/entity-kind-induction.js), its own null and `passed`. Relationship types: each significant directed link typed by (kind(A) -> kind(B)); reported with counts, the reverse-direction count of the same pair of kinds, and examples (display names for reading only).
Control: each evidence column shuffled independently (seed 20261118); also a control on the links: the same pass-2 run with each referent's neighbours shuffled among referents.
Falsifier (in advance): fewer than 3 kinds pass; or the control on the links finds as many kinds as the real run; or no relationship type has >= 10 links; or every kind's parameters are one dimension (e.g. only bound degree). Prominence leakage is reported, not a falsifier.
Not claimed: that these are the cube's, Aristotle's or Tinbergen's kinds; other languages; held-out; the coref prior (still unprimed).
