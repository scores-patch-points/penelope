# PREREG-CAUSES: do Aristotle's four causes emerge from co-arrival structure? (filed before it was run). EXPLORATORY, English, one corpus.
User steer: dependency chains matter; cross-section the EO cube with Aristotle's four causes; find these relationship types EMERGENTLY.
Operational signals (all computed from arrivals, no words): per ordered pair (A, B), A before B in the majority of their same-sentence co-mentions:
 EFFICIENT (what produces what, a temporal order): transfer-entropy asymmetry TE(A->B) - TE(B->A) over sentence-indicator series (khora binding.js transferEntropy).
 MATERIAL (what it is part of / made of: the Wilber destroy test): containment asymmetry c(A in B) - c(B in A), c(X in Y) = share of the articles containing X that also contain Y.
 FORMAL (what kind it is: shared structure): Jaccard similarity of the two entities' co-mention neighbourhoods (the entities they co-occur with in a sentence, excluding each other).
 FINAL (what it is for / where chains end): sink-ness of B = (in-degree - out-degree) / (in + out) in the directed graph of ordered same-sentence co-mentions.
Validation labels (NOT used to compute the signals; declared seeds, not learned): the connective words between the two mentions: efficient = built|founded|established|created|formed|designed|invented|discovered|constructed|named after|by; material = made of|consists|composed|comprising|comprises|includes|contains|divided into|part of|member of|within; formal = is a|is an|are a|was a|type of|kind of|known as|called|officially; final = for|in order to|so that|purpose|serves|used to|used for|aimed. A pair takes the class if exactly one class's cue occurs in the connective of at least one co-mention and no other class does; otherwise it is unlabelled and dropped.
Test: nearest-centroid classification of the labelled pairs from the four z-scored signals, leave-one-out; compared with (a) the majority-class rate and (b) the same classifier on frequency features only (log counts of A, B and co-mentions); permutation null over labels (1000 draws, seed 20261118).
Falsifier (in advance): if the four-signal accuracy does not exceed max(majority rate, frequency-only accuracy) by at least 0.05 with permutation p < 0.05, the four causes do not emerge from co-arrival structure at this scale and the cross-section needs a different instrument (words, or a deeper reader).
Not claimed: that the cue words are the truth of the causes (they are a validation lens with known weaknesses: "by", "of", "for" are polysemous); anything about the cube cells (the cross-section with the 27 phaseposts is the next step); fr/de; held-out.
