Nearest-centroid leave-one-out accuracy: four signals 0.507; frequency features only 0.538; signals + frequency 0.538; majority-class rate 0.525; permutation p = 0.001 (the permuted-label classifier scores below the majority rate, so the p only says the signals carry some label information).
FALSIFIER HIT: the four-signal accuracy (0.507) does not exceed the majority rate (0.525) or the frequency-only accuracy (0.538) by 0.05. At this scale and with these four signals, the four causes do NOT emerge as separable classes.
What did show structure (means per cue class): containment asymmetry separates the FORMAL cue class (is a / known as / called) from the others: 0.597 vs -0.048 (efficient) and 0.101 (material); neighbourhood similarity is highest for efficient (0.300) and final (0.397, n = 6) and lowest for material (0.172). Transfer-entropy asymmetry is ~0 for all (0.0005 to -0.0006): temporal order of sentence arrivals carries no signal for the efficient/material/formal distinction here, so the "efficient = what comes first" operationalisation is not supported.
Caveats: cue words are a weak validation lens ("by", "of", "for" are polysemous, and a pair needs exactly one cue class, which drops most pairs); final has n = 6; the four signals are my operationalisations, not khora's; one language; one genre (encyclopedic leads, which are heavy on is-a and located-in); the entity register is a stand-in; no held-out.
Reading: a single containment signal already separates "what kind it is" (formal) from the rest, which is consistent with the Pattern grain and the destroy-test view of dependence, but the full four-way cross-section does not emerge from arrival structure alone on this material. A different instrument is needed for efficient/material/final: the words between the mentions, a deeper reader, or text where causes are explicit (histories, recipes, mechanisms, legal and contract text).
