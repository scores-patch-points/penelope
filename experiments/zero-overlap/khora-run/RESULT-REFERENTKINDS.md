# RESULT-REFERENTKINDS: kinds of referent and of relationship between referents. PREREG-REFERENTKINDS sha 76b996e5ada358ba. EXPLORATORY, English, 321 Wikipedia leads. referent-kinds.mjs; leads-en-div.refkinds.json.
Level correction first (user): entities are REFERENTS (khora's cast), not strings. Strings only resolve a relation end to its referent.
Material: khora cast = 796 referents (790 with >= 2 mentions); 6,435 relations of which only 262 (4%) have BOTH ends resolvable to a referent.
RESULT: khora's kind inducer finds 7 referent kinds, all passing its own null (min size 14). Control (each evidence column shuffled independently): 1 kind. So the structure is real beyond the column marginals; the string-level runs found 0-2.
What the kinds are: six small kinds (30-34 referents each) are defined by tiers of how much the text talks about the referent (mentions, frames, span, naming share): a SALIENCE GRADIENT; k0 (high salience) reads as polities and peoples (Equatorial Guinea, Polish, AU, UN, Portugal, Pakistan, Byzantine Empire, Persian, India, Switzerland, NATO); the low-salience kinds mix people, places, taxa and institutions (Moshoeshoe, Riken, Spermophilus, Macaca, Mongolian, South Korea...). One kind (k6, 407 referents, 51%) is the remainder: dominant predicate class verb, low agency, low coupling.
Relationship types between kinded referents: 254 of 262 resolved edges, 251 of them inside k6 -> k6 (191 verb, 51 locative preposition, 11 other preposition, 1 copula). No relationship kinds emerge: the typing needs kinds on both ends, and almost no relations resolve to referent ends.
FALSIFIER: (1) >= 3 kinds pass: met (7). (2) control finds fewer: met (1 vs 7). (3) every kind defined by one dimension: PARTLY HIT: six of seven kinds are the salience gradient (mention/frame/span/naming-share tiers move together); only k6 is defined by relational and agentive features. Referent kinds exist, but mostly of "how prominent in the text", not of "what sort of thing".
Reading: at the referent level the inducer works (7 kinds vs 0-2 on strings, 1 on the control), but the evidence is dominated by prominence because the relational evidence is nearly empty (relN low for every referent: 4% of relations resolve at both ends). Kinds of relationship between referents cannot emerge until the relations are between referents.
Next: use the binding layer's links (referent-to-referent, null-tested, directed) as the relational evidence (a referent's bound neighbours, direction asymmetry, kinds of neighbours), drop the salience tiers from the evidence or hold them out, then type relationships as (kind, kind) pairs; and run it with a coref prior so pronouns and descriptors resolve.
