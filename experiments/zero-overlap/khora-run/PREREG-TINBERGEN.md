# PREREG-TINBERGEN: do Tinbergen's four questions emerge from co-arrival structure? (filed before it was run). EXPLORATORY, English, one corpus.
User steer: "try Tinbergen". Tinbergen's four questions about a trait: MECHANISM (how it works now: proximate, present), ONTOGENY (how it develops within an individual: proximate, historical), FUNCTION (what it is for: ultimate, present), PHYLOGENY (how it came to be across generations: ultimate, historical). A 2x2: proximate vs ultimate (scale: the individual vs the lineage/kind) crossed with present vs historical (time).
Corpus: English leads from articles.json, 40 per group, 10 groups (rivers, Alpine mountains, Nobel physicists, UN states, chemical elements, European mammals, Verdi operas, European languages, Impressionist painters, planets, Roman emperors where available), seed 20261118: a more diverse genre than the region leads of PREREG-CAUSES (people, species, languages, objects).
Signals (computed without the cue words): per ordered pair (A before B in the majority of same-sentence co-mentions): YEAR share = share of the pair's co-mention sentences that contain a 4-digit year 1000-2029 (a time anchor: historical); BREADTH = log(1 + number of distinct articles in which the pair co-mentions) (scale: one individual vs a kind); CONTAINMENT asymmetry c(A in B) - c(B in A) over articles; NEIGHBOUR similarity (Jaccard of co-mention neighbourhoods).
Validation labels (declared seeds, never used for the signals; no cue contains a year): mechanism = caused by|driven by|due to|as a result|through|via|mechanism|produces|operates|works by|flows; ontogeny = grew|developed|matured|childhood|early life|began as|became|over time|youth|born|raised|educated; function = for|in order to|serves|purpose|used to|function|role|allows|enables|so that|designed to; phylogeny = descended|evolved|ancestor|derived from|originated|lineage|predecessor|descendant|formerly|historically|origin|ancient|medieval|dynasty. A pair takes the class if exactly one class's cue appears in the connective of a co-mention (<= 8 words between adjacent mentions, no sentence punctuation); else dropped.
Test, as in PREREG-CAUSES: nearest-centroid classification of the labelled pairs from the four z-scored signals, leave-one-out; baselines: majority-class rate and a frequency-only classifier (log counts of A, B, co-mentions); permutation null (1000, seed 20261118). Also the two axes alone: present vs historical (mechanism+function vs ontogeny+phylogeny) and proximate vs ultimate (mechanism+ontogeny vs function+phylogeny), same baselines.
Falsifier (in advance): the four-class accuracy must exceed max(majority, frequency-only) by at least 0.05 with permutation p < 0.05; or, on an axis, the same margin: if neither the four-class nor either axis clears it, the Tinbergen cross-section does not emerge from co-arrival structure on this material either.
Not claimed: that cue words are the truth of the questions; the cube cross-section; other languages; held-out.
