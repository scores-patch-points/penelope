# RESULT-KINDS: what kinds of relationship exist in the wild? PREREG-KINDS sha 4155e5780f3d9244 + ADDENDUM-1/2. EXPLORATORY. Code kinds-induce.mjs, kinds-induce-agg.mjs; outputs kinds-*.json.
Material: 6,435 relation triples khora's reader found in 321 English Wikipedia leads (14 article groups). Instrument: khora's own kind inducer (kernel/entity-kind-induction.js::induceEntityKindCandidates, called as the-fold/kinds.js calls it), its own cohesion null and `passed` flag, no threshold of mine.
RESULTS
- V-A (instance = entity, base structural features; 3,000 random relations): 0 kinds, 0 basins (28 parameters).
- V-B (+ subject and object head words; 3,000 random relations): 0 kinds, 0 basins (30 parameters).
- V-C (kinds of RELATIONSHIP: entity = a predicate with >= 8 occurrences, evidence = the features of the ends it connects; 192 predicates): 1 kind passes (167 predicates, p = 0.0078); parameters mixed (objDigit yes and no, objLead prep, subjClass name and common): one blob holding 87% of the predicates, no separation. Cells of its 3,436 member occurrences: SIG·Figure 248, CON·Figure 22, DEF·Figure 43, SYN·Figure 29, SEG·Figure 8, NUL·Ground 15, SIG·Ground 7, SIG·Pattern 5, SYN·Ground 1, DEF·Ground 1, contested 284, gap 2,773 (10 distinct cells of 27 reached).
- V-D (kinds of BEING: entity = a subject head word with >= 8 occurrences, evidence = its predicates and object lead; 79 entities): 1 kind passes (78 entities, p = 0.0155): again one blob.
FALSIFIER HIT (fewer than 3 kinds pass; the one that does is not separable): relationship kinds do not emerge from this thin material through khora's inducer, at the instance level or the predicate level. The inducer is not broken (it found a kind in the PREREG-style statements case it was built for, per kinds.js); this relation material is too uniform and too sparse: the reader's "verb" is mostly a preposition, 192 predicates reach 8 occurrences, and the features are generic.
Reading: the relationships that exist in the wild, as this reader sees them, do not separate into kinds on structural evidence alone. Together with the earlier results (Aristotle and Tinbergen cue classes not separable from arrival signals) this says the typology has to come from a richer instrument: a reader with real predicates (verb-argument structure, roles, tense), or evidence that is not only the surface of the two ends. 
Not tested: other languages; the clause corpus of the lexical-analysis repo (7,808 clauses, with real main verbs and Claude/GPT three-question labels); kinds induced over the binding layer's links; held-out.
Caveat: 10 of 27 cells reached when 2,773 of 3,436 predicate occurrences are typed gaps (the act prior knows verbs, not prepositions).
