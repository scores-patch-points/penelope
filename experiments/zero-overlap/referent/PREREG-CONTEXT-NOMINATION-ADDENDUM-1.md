This addendum states how the harness was BUILT on DEV (referent-dev only; no DEV number is a claim) and fixes the clarifications below. It changes no hypothesis and no falsifier.
## What DEV showed about the design (not claims)
- Name-bearing sentences are the query's own source for N1 and N3, so every new arm REMOVES the name-bearing sentences from its ranking (as row 30's EMBED-C did). Row 30's EMBED-N (N0) did NOT remove them, which makes its ALL stratum and its precision@10 not comparable with the arms (the name-bearing sentences are gold SAME). So an arm N0x (EMBED-N with the same removal) is ADDED as a control, and F1 is judged against the BETTER of N0 and N0x on ZERO-CLEAN (a stricter reading of F1).
- F6 is reported TWICE: LITERALLY against N0 (as registered), and like-for-like against N0x. The literal comparison is structurally unfair (N0's top 10 contains the name-bearing gold sentences); it is reported as it comes, with this note.
- F2 applies to M1 as well as N1 and N3 ("for any arm"): the swap control for M1 is the fold built from another target's name tokens.
- U1 is computed for each of N1, N2, N3 (each interleaved with M1 at equal size K = 10); F4 is judged against each union's better member.
## Clarifications of the registration's text (stated, not tuned)
- "khora's text-cuts": the gold passages are indexed by the set's own sentence cuts (lib.build_doc), so the paraphrase and the windows use THOSE cuts; khora's cuts are an observation of the same kind but would need a re-indexing of the gold. Not a hypothesis change.
- "window w = 3 ... w in {1, 5} reported": w is the NUMBER of sentences in the window, centred (w = 1 is the sentence alone, w = 3 previous, itself, next, w = 5 two each side); the score of a window is assigned to its centre sentence.
- Name-bearing = every content token of the name (no stop word, more than one character) is among the sentence's tokens: set membership over tokens made by walking the characters (alphanumeric runs, Unicode NFKD with marks dropped, lower case). No regular expression and no model in the builder (tested: test_context_nom.py asserts neither module imports re).
- The fold (fold_nom.py) is khora's round-2 design: PPMI context vectors over +-4 words for word types with at least 5 occurrences, cosine with every vocabulary word, the median and 1.4826 x MAD of all those cosines as the unit, fold iff robust z >= sqrt(2 ln V). Declared deviations: NO lemma lexicon; the background is the whole set's text (target included; text only, never links); a degenerate spread (MAD 0) folds nothing. On DEV the mean class size of a name token was about 84 words (a very loose fold at this background size): reported as a finding about M1, not tuned away.
- ZERO-CLEAN = gold sentences in ZERO that zero_clean.related (declared normaliser of ERRATUM 2: NFKD with marks dropped, plural fold on words of 4 or more letters, equal token, shared 5-letter prefix, edit distance 1) does not relate to the name. A query whose name has no name-bearing sentence is SKIPPED and counted (DEV: 81 of 486).
- Bootstrap: over documents, 2,000 draws, seed 20261119. A stratum with fewer than 30 gold passages is UNJUDGEABLE (DEV's ZERO-CLEAN had 25: unjudgeable on DEV, which is why the fresh set exists).
- Embedder: all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (referent/models), sentence-transformers 6.3.0, torch 2.15.0, transformers 5.23.0, numpy 2.5.5, CPU, in a venv outside the repo; installed with the owner's yes of 2026-11-19.
