# compare: the human procedure for "do these two statements, in two documents, say the same thing relevant to the question?" (built 2026-11-19)
`compare.mjs` implements the steps of THEORY-IDENTITY.md sections 14 to 16: the question is declared first; each statement is UNWRAPPED in its paragraph (language declared and heard; a mismatch is refused); who and what are BOUND (khora referents; an unbound filler never matches); each is REDUCED to claims (relation, ARG0, ARG1, polarity; saturated means both roles are bound); every difference is CLASSIFIED (Conflict, Silence, Resolution, or HOLE for untied, unsaturated and undeclared; the accidental set is NFKC and spacing, case kept); only differences on the question's concerns count; the VERDICT is Other, Same or Hold with a hunger list; the TRAIL is a byte address per statement that re-opens or refuses, and the verdict is an entry in the pharos shape whose id recomputes.
Rules it keeps: a Resolution never convicts (Same at the coarse grain, Hold at the fine one); a swapped-role Conflict only where the relation is DECLARED asymmetric; a different object of a relation not declared one-valued is a HOLE (possibly another event); an unsaturated claim supports but never convicts and is never strong evidence; by, at and language are declared, never defaulted. Cross-document comparison is a declared act (the question names the referent), not ground overlap.
**Tests** (`compare.test.mjs`, node --test, needs khora and its priors): 13 of 13 pass. Mutation check: breaking the code three ways (a denial counted as agreement, a conflict that no longer convicts, an unsaturated agreement counted as strong) is caught by the tests; the third was NOT caught until I added a test, so the first suite was weaker than it looked.
**Smoke test on real statement pairs** (`make_pairs.py`, `smoke.mjs`; the 30 DEV articles; EXPLORATORY, no claim): 41 true pairs (a gold sentence holding the name against a gold sentence of the same referent without it) and 41 impostor pairs (against a gold sentence of another target of the same article). Every one of the 82 comes back Hold: Same 0, Other 0, Hold 41 and 41. The comparator makes no wrong call, but it makes no useful one. Claims about the referent exist on the A side (which holds the name) for only 5 of 41 true pairs, on the B side for 3 of 41, on both sides for 0. Saturated claims about the referent: A 1, B 0.
**Why.** Not a bug in the matching (I inspected nine A statements): the link targets that the gold marks as "mentions" are often concepts (Limestone, Association football, Dollar coin) or sit in a prepositional phrase ("against Serbs", "faced League One side AFC Bournemouth"), not as subject or object of the clause; and the name-free B statements are pronoun or description statements with nothing bound to the referent. The reader supplies subject and object roles only, so an oblique mention (and the time, place and manner adjuncts of law L13) is invisible, and there is no coreference to bind "he".
**What it means.** The comparator is sound and sound by construction (it says Hold when it cannot tell), but with the reader as it is, it can answer only where both statements put the referent in a subject or object role by name. That predicts a NO-GO for the retrieval claim of PREREG-E2E (gate G2: at least 0.30 of queries with a SAME). A cheaper precheck before spending the new set: the share of nominated gold passages that carry any claim about the referent. The smoke numbers say about 0.07 (3 of 41).
**Needs from the reader, in order:** oblique roles (place, time, manner, the object of a preposition); the descriptor lane and pronoun presence to bind "he" and "the old man" (khora has both; not wired here); tense as a time anchor; attribution frames. Each is a typed gap in the output now (`attribution_not_run`, `time_not_wired`).

## The log and understanding that runs backward (log.mjs, built 2026-11-19; THEORY-IDENTITY.md section 17)
`log.mjs` puts compare under an append-only log of pharos-shaped entries (cell, kind, addr, body, of, basis, seen, by, at, id; id = sha256 of the canonical entry; `seen` computed, `at` declared). Kinds and cells: verdict EVA·Figure, merge REC·Figure, apart DEF·Figure, revision REC·Figure, admit EVA·Figure, retract NUL·Figure.
- **Identity events are witnessed.** A merge is a must-link and an apart a cannot-link; neither is recorded without a witness. They apply to every name that is an alias of a member (the reader says "Bell" for "Marshall Bell"); a must-link component that holds a cannot-link pair is held WHOLE, whatever the order of the links (tested on three orders).
- **Hindsight.** After an identity event, khora's `kernel/hindsight.js` returns which earlier verdicts rest on the re-addressed ids, with distance and a rank against a null of random touched-sets. Each is re-run under the identity known at the event. A changed verdict appends a new verdict (`of` the old verdict and the event) and a REVISION; the old entry is never rewritten.
- **Hindsight proposes, foresight tests.** A revision is only PROPOSED until a foresight gate admits it (`admit` entry, admitted true or false, both on the log); with no gate the standing does not move. The gate is a function the caller supplies; here it is a stub in the tests. Defining a real foresight measure for a passage verdict is still open (khora's rounds 8 and 9 measure surprise on pronoun and description mentions).
- **A witnessed Other is revoked only by retracting a witness.** A revision away from Other stays "held-by-witness" until the verdict or an identity entry it rests on is retracted.
- **The cursor.** `standingAt(log, n)` projects the standing as of the first n entries: Hold at cursor 1, Same at the end. A verdict whose referent was re-addressed and not yet reconsidered is flagged `stale`. A Same weakened by a later cannot-link is flagged `break`.
- **Replay.** `Log.fromJSONL` reloads and verifies every id; a drifted entry is refused.
**Tests:** `compare.test.mjs` 13 and `log.test.mjs` 9, 22 of 22 pass. Mutation check: five deliberate breakages of the log (revisions applying without a gate, an Other changing without a retracted witness, a drifted entry loading, an unwitnessed merge recorded, a held component still merging) are all caught; the last was NOT caught by the first suite and needed a direct test of the component hold.
**Not done:** a real foresight measure; stability under append on real documents (N4, pre-registered); the reader's oblique roles and coreference (the smoke result above stands: on real pairs every verdict is still Hold); transfer across documents.

## The foresight gate (foresight.mjs, built 2026-11-19; THEORY-IDENTITY.md section 18)
`makeForesight(log, eventIndex, docs)` returns the gate `reconsider` takes: the surprise an identity event removes from the mentions AFTER the cut (khora's round-8 reader and constants), against a null of random merges, admitted only above median + 1.4826 x MAD; typed gaps (no later material, an underpowered null, a cannot-link) admit nothing. 6 tests, mutation-checked five ways. Synthetic documents only: no result about real text yet.
