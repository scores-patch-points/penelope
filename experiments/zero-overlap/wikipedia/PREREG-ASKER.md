# PREREG-ASKER: equivalence as "the difference, for the asker, is below the asker's difference-that-makes-a-difference", and the asymptotic approach (filed before it was run). EXPLORATORY: every set below was already read by an earlier frame, so no number here is HELD-OUT or FRESH (ledger rule 4).
User (2026-11-19): "2 things are equivalent when the difference between them for the person asking is less than a difference that makes a difference; that's how we get the asymptotic approach."
Definition used: equivalent(A, B | asker) iff D_asker(A, B) <= delta_asker, where the ASKER declares (i) which dimensions matter to them (a mask) and (ii) delta, the size of difference that makes a difference to them, per dimension. D_asker is the largest normalised difference over the masked dimensions present on BOTH sides; a pair with no comparable masked dimension has no verdict (a hole, never a vote).
Dimensions, from the FROZEN extractors (no change): area (relative difference of km2; PREREG-CORROB extractor), born and died year (absolute years; PREREG-CLAIMS extractor), population with a STATED year on both sides (relative difference after allowing drift of 0.03 per year apart; PREREG-REFERENT extractor; untimed claims excluded as untrustworthy per the ledger).
Asymptotic approach: sweep delta DOWN a declared ladder of six levels, aligned across dimensions: relative delta 0.50, 0.25, 0.10, 0.05, 0.02, 0.01 and year delta 50, 20, 5, 2, 1, 0. The equivalent set shrinks monotonically by construction (a max rule); the question is whether it shrinks toward the TRUE pairs: at each level report survival of true pairs (the article and its other-language counterpart) and of impostors (the article against each other-language lead of the same category), and the persistence gap (true survival minus impostor survival).
Two askers, declared now: IDENTITY (mask: area, born, died) and SIZE (mask: population). Report for how many pairs the two askers' verdicts differ at the loosest and the tightest level.
Data: fr and de, fresh sets 1-4 and the dev/held pair files; true pairs and scene-mate impostors (seed 20261118, at most 30 impostors per article).
Falsifiers (in advance): (1) for the IDENTITY asker, no level has true survival >= 0.80 with impostor survival <= 0.10: the threshold rule does not separate; (2) the maximum persistence gap over the ladder is < 0.30: no asymptotic approach shown; (3) the SIZE asker's verdicts equal the IDENTITY asker's on every pair: the asker adds nothing.
Not claimed: that these dimensions are the right ones; any held-out claim; population drift 0.03 is the frames' declared value, not tuned.
