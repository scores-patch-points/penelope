# PREREG-CORROB: corroboration by a second quantity (area), and area as the tie (filed before scoring)
Idea: a population number cannot be tied to its subject by a text window (PREREG-TIE). But a second quantity about the same entity can tie it: two different administrative divisions rarely share an area. So AREA does two jobs: (a) an identifier, like a birth year: if both leads state an area and they AGREE, the articles describe one entity, and a population mismatch is then a leaf dispute (a sub-entity number or a real disagreement), not a sign of a different subject; (b) a corroborating falsifier: a pair is FALSIFIED only if the population disputes (R2, stated time, as frozen) AND the areas dispute.
Area extractor A (declared, nothing learned): the first number followed by a squared-length unit (km2, km², sq km, sq mi, square kilometres/miles, kilomètres carrés, km carrés, Quadratkilometer, qkm); km² preferred over sq mi; sq mi x 2.58999; thousands separators and a decimal point as in the population parser; a scale word "million" multiplies by 1e6. A bare "km" (a distance) never counts.
Area comparison (declared): AGREE if the relative difference <= 0.10; DISPUTE if > 0.25; between is UNCLEAR (a hole: land vs total area, different definitions).
Verdicts per pair: AREA-TIED (areas agree); CORROBORATED-FALSIFIED (population dispute AND area dispute); AREA-FALSIFIED (areas dispute, whatever the population: reported separately as a falsifier in its own right); else no verdict.
Stage A, DEV: F1 + F2 + F3 pooled (fr 685 / de 622 pairs; already used for the population frames, but the area extractor has never been run on them). Read what A extracts and every true-pair area dispute; ONE erratum allowed; then FREEZE.
Stage B, FRESH F4: new categories, declared before the fetch, chosen by member count only; run once.
Reported on each set, fr and de: coverage (both areas present); TRUE pairs: area agree / unclear / dispute; SUITOR trials (same category, both areas present): agree (coincidence) / unclear / dispute; the population-dispute x area-dispute lift among suitor trials (independence: lift near 1 means independent; high means the two quantities fail together); true pairs rescued from a population dispute by an area agreement; corroborated-falsified rate on true pairs and on suitors; Wilson 95% intervals.
Falsifiers (in advance), on Stage B: (1) true-pair area DISPUTE rate with n >= 30: upper end of the 95% interval > 0.10: area is an unreliable identifier; (2) true-pair area AGREE rate < 0.80: area misses true pairs; (3) suitor area AGREE (coincidence) rate > 0.10: area is too weak an identifier among similar divisions; (4) both-area coverage < 0.10: too narrow. If (1)-(3) hold, area stands as a falsifier and a tie; population stays a leaf.
Not tested: other quantities (density is NOT independent: it is population / area); a parser-based tie; non-division referents.

## ERRATUM-1 (Stage A, the one allowed): an extraction bug, not a result. Frozen after this.
First dev run: true-pair area "disputes" were 18 of 56 (fr) and 40 of 121 (de), and nearly all were my parser, not the articles: "7,007.54 square kilometres" was read as 54 or 8 (a thousands separator followed by a decimal tail broke the number pattern); German "35.751,65 km²" (dot thousands, comma decimal) as 751; thin and other Unicode spaces as thousands separators ("84 036" read as 36); Flevoland 1,410 vs 2,412 is a genuine land-vs-total-area difference and stays. Fix (frozen): the number pattern accepts a thousands-grouped integer with any whitespace, comma or dot separators followed by an optional decimal tail ([.,] and digits), or a plain decimal; the last separator is a decimal point when not followed by exactly three digits at the end. Nothing else changes: units, comparison thresholds (agree <= 0.10, dispute > 0.25), verdicts, Stage B and the falsifiers are as filed.
