# PREREG-SOFTCHAIN: confirming the soft region+area chain on a FRESH set (filed before it was fetched or run)
Rule (frozen; from PREREG-HOLARCHY ADDENDUM-3, written post-hoc on F1-F4, so this set is its first honest test). For a scene (a category) and a claimant p (English article) whose English and other-language areas and both containers are present: A(p) = the scene members with an other-language area within 0.10 of p's area (the frozen area extractor of PREREG-CORROB, relative difference); M(p) = the best-matching region sets (PREREG-HOLARCHY ADDENDUM-1, frozen container regexes, computed over scene members with a container on both sides). Answer set = A(p) if |A(p)| <= 1, else A(p) intersected with M(p) when that is non-empty, else A(p). The truth (k = p) is never used to build any set.
Fresh set F5: categories chosen by member count only (no lead read), disjoint from F1-F4: States of Nigeria (41), Regions of Tanzania (35), Provinces of Bulgaria (32), Provinces of Ecuador (29), Regions of Madagascar (28), Provinces of the Democratic Republic of the Congo (28), Provinces of Papua New Guinea (25), Counties of Sweden (25), Provinces of China (25), Governorates of Iraq (23), Districts of Portugal (21), Counties of Estonia (21), Regions of the Czech Republic (21), Provinces of Laos (20), Departments of Honduras (20), Regions of Namibia (18); direct pages only; scene = category; fr and de counterparts by interlanguage links; leads truncated to 900 characters; pages in F1-F4 dropped. I do not read the fetched leads before the single run.
Reported (fr, de separately; a language with fewer than 30 eligible claimants is reported as too small): TRUTH KEPT in the answer set (soft chain) versus area alone; mean answer-set size for both; UNIQUE-AND-CORRECT (answer set = the truth alone) for both with Wilson 95% intervals; the HARD chain (SAME only if in M(p) and areas agree) TP and false accept for reference.
Falsifiers (in advance): (1) truth kept by the soft chain < 0.90; (2) unique-and-correct for the soft chain not above area alone with non-overlapping 95% intervals; (3) mean answer-set size not smaller than area alone; (4) eligible claimants < 30 (too small to judge).
Not claimed: anything about pairs without both an area and a container (they stay gaps); non-division referents; other languages.
