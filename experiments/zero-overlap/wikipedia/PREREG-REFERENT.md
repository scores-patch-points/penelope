# PREREG-REFERENT: a referent-aware population extractor (R2), and a FRESH held-out (filed before it was scored)
Why: PREREG-TIME showed untimed population claims are mostly not clean statements of the entity's own population (area figures, sub-populations, the capital's number). R2 tries to read the number's referent with stricter rules. Nothing is learned.
R2 rules (all declared now): (1) window 50 characters (was 80) between keyword and number; (2) parse as frozen (a comma or dot is a decimal when a scale word follows); (3) EXCLUDE a number followed within 12 characters by %, /, km, km2, km², sq, square, mi, ha, hectare(s), m², Quadratkilometer, Hektar, or preceded within 8 characters by a currency sign or code; (4) EXCLUDE a number with a subset marker BEFORE it within 30 characters (dont, of which, including, davon, darunter, inklusive) or a capital marker AFTER it within 30 characters (in der Hauptstadt, dans la capitale, in the capital), or an urban-area marker within 30 characters either side (aire urbaine, urban area, metropolitan, agglomeration, Grossraum); (5) the old blanket "capital"/"Hauptstadt" exclusion is DROPPED (it would remove real claims in capital cities and broke San Marino through "dont" AFTER the number); (6) choose the nearest remaining number to a population keyword.
Comparison: exactly as PREREG-FLUENT / compareClaims (tol 0.05, maxRate 0.03). A claim with a stated year uses [year, year]; a claim with none is UNTIMED. For the question this prereg asks, untimed claims are compared with inferredMayDispute = true in the diagnostic only (never as a verdict): the would-dispute rate on true pairs measures whether untimed claims became trustworthy.
Stage A, DEV (acts <= 3): run R2 vs the frozen v1; read every true-pair dispute; ERRATUM allowed; then FREEZE. Disclosure: this is the third revision tuned on this dev data.
Stage B, FRESH HELD-OUT (never seen): new categories chosen now: English Wikipedia Category:Capitals in Europe, Capitals in Asia, Capitals in Africa, Capitals in South America, Capitals in North America, and States of the United States; each member's English lead and its fr and de counterparts by Wikipedia's own interlanguage links; scene = category; suitors = the other members of the same category. Cities are a new referent (city proper vs metro), so this is a harder, fresh test. I do not read the fetched leads before the single run. Fetched via the query API in batches; pairs where the other-language article is absent are dropped.
Reported: coverage, true pairs (stated-both: agree / compatible / dispute; untimed-involved: agree / compatible / would-dispute), suitors (dispute rate among stated-both; would-dispute among untimed-involved), fr and de separately.
Falsifiers (in advance), all on the FRESH set: (1) stated-both true-pair dispute rate among comparable > 0.10; (2) untimed-involved true-pair would-dispute rate > 0.10 (untimed claims remain untrustworthy; they stay blocked); (3) stated-both suitor dispute rate < 0.80; (4) coverage < 0.03.
Not tested: other fluents; a rewriting adversary; Russian.

## ERRATUM-1 (Stage A done; rules FROZEN, no change). ## ADDENDUM-1: second fresh held-out F2 (filed before it was fetched; extractor R2 FROZEN, unchanged)
Purpose: widen the fresh test past the 100-pair floor and add a different referent (administrative divisions). F1 (capitals + US states, 61 fr / 68 de) stays as run. F2 uses categories disjoint from F1 and from the dev sample, chosen by member COUNT only (no lead read): English Wikipedia Provinces of Italy (94), Prefectures of Japan (58), Cantons of Switzerland (41), States and union territories of India (38), Regions of France (35), Provinces of Argentina (29), States of Germany (19), Provinces of the Netherlands (17); direct pages only; scene = category; suitors = the other members of the same category; fr and de counterparts by interlanguage links; leads truncated to 900 characters. No rule, window, tolerance or marker is changed. I do not read the fetched leads before the single run.
Reported: F2 alone, and F1 + F2 pooled, each with Wilson 95% intervals on the true-pair stated-time dispute rate, the stated-time suitor dispute rate, and the untimed would-dispute rate.
Falsifiers (same as Stage B), judged on F2 alone and on the pool: (1) stated-both true dispute > 0.10 (judged on the interval's upper end: hit if the upper end of the 95% interval exceeds 0.10 AND n >= 30; otherwise "n too small"); (2) untimed would-dispute > 0.10 (same reading); (3) stated-both suitor dispute < 0.80; (4) coverage < 0.03.
Not tested: other fluents; a rewriting adversary; other languages.
