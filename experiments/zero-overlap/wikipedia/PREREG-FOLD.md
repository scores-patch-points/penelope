# PREREG-FOLD: the relation fold applied to the frozen extractors (filed before it was run). EXPLORATORY: no fresh data left that I have not read; this is an integration check, not a new held-out claim.
What: both leads of a pair are read by the FROZEN extractors (area A from PREREG-CORROB; born/died years from PREREG-CLAIMS) into relationship claims {end1 = the pair's asserted subject (anchor "X": the pairing asserts one subject, the falsification attempt), label area | born | died, end2 = the number or year, addr = the title and side}. Population is entered as a LEAF only. Each side is folded (foldRelations, ground "lead-section") and compared with sameFold (roles: area, born, died = root; population = leaf; tol 0.10 for area, 0 for years via exact match; minAgree 1).
Verdicts: same (>= 1 root relationship agrees, none disputes) / other (a root dispute) / gap (no shared root relationship). Compared on F1-F4 (fr, de): true pairs, and suitor pairs (the article against each scene-mate's other-language lead).
Reported: true pairs same / other / gap; suitor pairs same / other / gap; same among all suitor trials (false accept) and, on pairs with a root relation, the Wilson interval. Gap is never counted as a yes.
Falsifier (written first): a suitor 'same' rate (false accept) above 0.05 on pairs with a shared root relation, or a true-pair 'other' rate above 0.10 with n >= 30.
Not tested: relations other than these; zero-overlap sameness on text with no anchors (the gap case is exactly where this fold has nothing to say).
