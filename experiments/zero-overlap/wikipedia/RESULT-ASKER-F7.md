## Reading
The criterion works as designed on strong identity facts, and the dependency order helps: the death asker needs born upstream and rejects natural impostors almost entirely (0.002-0.003) while true pairs stay at 0.99-1.00. But (1) every surface dimension is forgeable by an impostor that has read the claimant: 0.92-1.00 equivalent, so the verdict must travel with the adversary rate and cannot stand alone; (2) the approach is the impostors' fall, not the true pairs' decay: true pairs are flat for the years (no approach among true pairs); (3) area is the weak veto here: true pairs are judged other 4 of 21 and 7 of 24 and coverage is 3%; (4) coverage is the binding limit (BIRTH 0.21 fr to 0.75 de, DEATH 0.17 to 0.50, PLACE 0.03).
