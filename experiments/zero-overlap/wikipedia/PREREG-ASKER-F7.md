## ERRATUM-1 (after the first scoring; the first scoring is FRESH and stands as run)
The first run's informed arm for PERSONS prepended "(BORN - DIED) " with bare years. The frozen life-span extractor only reads a first parenthesis with a DATE-LIKE pattern (a day-number beside a month word and a year, or a * or dagger marker), so that string was not read as a claim and the attack never forged anything for persons (informed equivalent rates 0.000-0.030 for BIRTH and DEATH are the failure of the attack's construction, not robustness). The PLACE attack ("Area: N km2. ") did work (area equivalence among verdict-bearing informed trials 60/64 fr, 48/52 de). Corrected attack, declared now: persons prepend "(1 January BORN – 1 January DIED) " (or "(born 1 January BORN) " when only a birth year is known). Re-scoring the informed arm on the same F7 set is a SECOND scoring of that arm: it is reported as REUSED, a separate ledger row; the first-scoring numbers are not replaced. Only the informed-persons arm is rerun; the TRUE and NATURAL arms, the askers, tolerances and falsifiers are unchanged. Also corrected in reporting: the informed equivalent rate in the first log divided by ALL informed arms including those where the asker has no verdict; the meaningful rate is among verdict-bearing informed trials, reported below.
