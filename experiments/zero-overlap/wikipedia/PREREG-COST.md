# PREREG-COST: forging cost of each frame (filed before scoring). DEV ONLY (fr, de act 3).
Cost of a frame for a claimant = the fewest tokens an informed adversary must append to one scene-mate's text (never changing its own text) for that candidate to pass the frame (score above the claimant's seeded null maximum and >= 1), by a greedy frame-specific attack; capped at 60; "unforgeable here" if the claimant's own material cannot reach the bar. The adversary knows the claimant's text, the frame and the induced lexicon.
Frames: overlap, usage (T_all), numerals, R_set (shared entities), R (shared ordered entity pairs; the adversary appends the claimant's entities in order). Attacks: overlap = append the claimant's non-stop tokens; usage = append one licensed partner per claimant probe token; numerals = append the claimant's numerals; R_set = append the claimant's entities; R = append the claimant's entity sequence in text order.
Reported per frame: share of claimants forgeable within 3, 10 and 30 tokens, median finite cost, share unforgeable, with the frame's own accuracy (TP, natural suitor-pass) beside it. Also the ladder: OR-ladder (any frame; cost = the minimum) and AND-ladder of overlap+usage+numerals (all three; cost = the sum), each with TP and natural FA.
Falsifiers (in advance): (1) if the most accurate frame (R_set or overlap, BA 0.95) is also the cheapest to forge, accuracy and forging cost trade off, and no single frame is both; (2) if the AND-ladder is still forgeable within 10 tokens for more than half the claimants, no combination of these surface frames resists forging; (3) if the AND-ladder true-pair rate falls below 0.20, its robustness is bought by losing the true pairs.
Not tested: adversaries that rewrite sentences or detect stuffing; ground or fact-level frames; held-out (consumed).
