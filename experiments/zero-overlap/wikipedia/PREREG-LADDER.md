# PREREG-LADDER: the integrated ladder, on a set nobody has read (filed before F6 was fetched or any frame was scored on it)
Why: every frame was tested alone. The question under the doctrine is the stack: what is the verdict of the frames that earned a place, with default Other, and how does it fare against an informed adversary? Nothing here is tuned; every frame, threshold and rule is frozen from earlier stamped work.
Frames (frozen): (1) AREA from PREREG-CORROB (extractor A, agree <= 0.10, dispute > 0.25); (2) NAMES from PREREG-BRIDGE (the number of identical link targets, accent- and case-folded, in the English and other-language lead's wikitext; pass = above the maximum of 50 length-matched null candidates, seed 20261118, and >= 1; stop = the 50 most frequent link targets per language in the F6 set itself, computed without labels); (3) TOKENS (plain shared 5-character prefixes, 50 frequent types removed per side, the same null).
Ladder verdict for the claimant p (English article) and a candidate q (an other-language article of the same category): OTHER if both areas are present and DISPUTE (a falsifier: veto); else SAME iff some frame in the order AREA-agree, NAMES, TOKENS passes for (p, q) AND no OTHER member of the category passes that same frame for p (the frame separates the candidate from its rivals; judgeFrames semantics; for a true pair the rivals are all scene-mates, for an impostor candidate the rivals are the other scene-mates); else GAP (reported, counted as not-same: default Other).
Fresh set F6 (categories chosen by member count only, none read, disjoint from F1-F5): Provinces of Mongolia (24), Regions of Kazakhstan (23), Departments of Uruguay (21), Provinces of the Dominican Republic (38), Provinces of Burkina Faso (47), Regions of Ethiopia (20), Districts of Uganda (127), Districts of Sri Lanka (28), Districts of Bhutan (22), Counties of Liberia (17), Counties of Albania (18), Regions of Uzbekistan (18). Direct pages; scene = category; fr and de counterparts by interlanguage links; plain-text leads truncated to 900 characters; lead wikitext (section 0) for the link targets. I do not read the fetched leads before the single run.
Reported (fr and de; strata ALL / LOW (tokens overlap <= 1) / ZERO): TP, impostor-SAME rate (false accept), gap rate, OTHER rate for the ladder; the same for each frame alone under the same null; which frame decided each SAME; Wilson 95% intervals.
ADVERSARY ARM (informed impostor): per claimant, one scene-mate candidate (first by sorted title) is edited by an adversary that knows the claimant and the frames: strategies NAMES (add k of the claimant's English link targets to the candidate's link set), AREA (set the candidate's area to the claimant's), TOKENS (add k of the claimant's non-stop tokens), MIX (all three, k split) with k = 3 and 10; the candidate's own content is never removed. Reported: the ladder's SAME rate for the edited candidate, and each frame's pass rate.
Falsifiers (in advance): (1) the ladder's balanced accuracy (TP vs false accept; gap counts as not-same) on ALL and on LOW must be at least the best single frame's; if not, stacking adds nothing; (2) the ladder's SAME rate for the MIX adversary at k = 10 must be <= 0.10; if not, the ladder is forgeable by a handful of appended tokens (expected); (3) fewer than 30 pairs in a stratum is "too small".
Disclosure: F6 is unread; the components were each held-out-tested on F5 and tuned on F1-F4. The combination rule, order and veto are declared here for the first time and not tuned on any data.
Not claimed: non-division referents; non-Latin scripts; anything khora's own referents would add (fr/de priors do not exist yet).

## ADDENDUM-1 (user, 2026-11-19: "for this perspective, there is no difference that makes a difference"). Filed before F6 was scored. Adds a PERSPECTIVE verdict beside the ladder; nothing about the ladder, the frames or F6 changes.
The same frames are re-expressed as the probes of ONE declared perspective, judged by judgeEquivalence (organs/perspective-eva.mjs, 5 tests; the Python mirror in ladder.py follows it line by line): perspective "reader of the same article". PROBES and their measured POWER (power = the impostor rejection rate measured on F1-F5, fixed here, never taken from F6): AREA (power 0.85; a difference that makes a difference is a relative difference > 0.25; <= 0.25 counts as no difference found); NAMES (power 0.96); TOKENS (power 0.91). NAMES and TOKENS are SUPPORT probes: they can only AGREE (the frame's pass rule against the claimant's null) or stay SILENT; failing to share names or words is never a difference that makes a difference (a lead may simply not link or repeat), so they cannot vote Different. A side that cannot answer a probe is silent. Verdict: different if an answered probe differs; equivalent if none differs and at least one answered probe with power agrees; gap otherwise (default Other: gap counts as not-same).
This is judged pair by pair (no rivals); the ladder keeps its rival rule. Reported for both: TP, false accept, gap, different; by stratum; and the adversary arm for both. Added falsifier (in advance): the perspective verdict's false-accept rate on F6 must not exceed the best single support probe's by more than 0.05 while its TP is at least that probe's; if a union of support probes only inflates false accepts, "equivalent" is claimed too cheaply and the probe set is not a perspective that earns the verdict.

## ADDENDUM-2 (user, 2026-11-19: a perspective is a terrain). Filed before F6 was scored; changes nothing about what is scored.
The perspective "reader of the same article" now DECLARES where it lives: terrain Lens (a specific standpoint reading; its verdicts are Lens events), frame "same article as another-language lead of the same category" (Atmosphere: the standard of meaning); its probes land on AREA -> Entity, NAMES -> Link, TOKENS -> Entity (the lead's surface). organs/perspective-eva.mjs now rejects a perspective that is not at an Interpretation terrain (Lens, Paradigm, Atmosphere) and treats perspectives at different terrains or frames as not comparable (7 tests).
