FINDINGS
1. Time is the bottleneck. Among covered pairs, 13 of 29 (fr) and 9 of 26 (de) true pairs, and 77% (fr) / 91% (de) of suitor trials, are BLOCKED because one side states a number with no as-of year. A fluent without its time cannot be judged: that is the dependency order you asked for, and the system correctly refused to guess. It is also why the frame is mostly silent.
2. Where the time is known, the verdict is useful: true pairs agree or are compatible (population differs because the dates differ) and impostors are rejected at 0.86-0.93. The drift term did work: Bahrain 1.59M (2024) vs 1.23M (2010) is compatible, not a dispute.
3. A genuine source disagreement exists and stays a dispute: Central African Republic 5.36M (2024) vs 6.1M (2023): 12% against the 8% the declared drift allows.
4. Referent is the hard part. Dev read-through fixed two extraction bugs (a comma read as a thousands mark before "million"; a capital's population read as the country's). The fix for the second (a subset-marker filter) then broke San Marino in dev: "34 132 habitants dont 5 927 étrangers" had its correct number skipped, so the citizens-abroad number 17 924 (2025) was compared with 34,042 and disputed. Held-out Benin: an EN number 43,484 (2026) vs 14,814,460 disputed (a wrong referent on the EN side; not inspected further). Rules written by reading dev are brittle against the referent problem.
5. Ordering disclosure: I ran the held-out in the same command as the post-erratum dev rerun, so the San Marino dispute was seen at the same time as the held-out result, not before it. The rules were frozen by ERRATUM-1 before the held-out run and were not changed afterwards; but I did not do the second read-through I had planned. Treat held-out as run once under frozen rules, with that ordering flaw.
6. Sensitivity (dev, transparency only): tol 0.02-0.10 and maxRate 0.01-0.05 move true-pair dispute rates (fr 0.06-0.36; de 0.00) and time-known suitor dispute rates (fr 0.87-0.97; de 0.73-0.96) but do not change the picture: the frame is accurate where time is known and silent otherwise.
Not tested: other fluents (office held, location at a time), the referent problem solved by anything but rules, a rewriting adversary, Russian.
