# PREREG-KHORA: khora's own referents as the document's referent graph (filed before it was run)
Source (the primary path, per the user's rule relayed by the coreference session): khora's reading, readWith(text, { language, referents: true, castIdentity: true }) from native/the-fold/read-core.mjs, run on a READ-ONLY SNAPSHOT of commit a061467 (branch round23) exported with git archive into this session's scratchpad (nothing in any khora worktree was touched or run). Languages: eng, fra, deu; the 900-character plain-text leads already in the pairs (F1-F5). Reader failures are kept as typed outcomes (no referents, error), never dropped silently; their share is reported per language.
Measures declared now:
(1) COVERAGE of the reader per language: share of leads with >= 1 referent; mean referents and edges per lead; typed gaps by kind.
(2) SUBJECT = the referent with the most mentions in the lead (ties: first). NEIGHBOURS(p) = the faces (accent-folded, lower-cased) of referents joined to the subject by at least one edge. 
(3) CONTAINER over referents (the L1 rule of PREREG-L1, with khora's neighbours in place of link strings): within a scene and language, df(f) = the number of members whose NEIGHBOURS hold face f; candidate if 2 <= df <= floor(n/2); container = the candidate with the highest df, ties to the one nearest the subject in the text.
(4) Cross-language structure test (as PREREG-HOLARCHY ADDENDUM-1, with these containers): destroy-set agreement of the within-language partitions against the shuffle null (300 shuffles, seed 20261118), over scenes with >= 8 members having a container on both sides; and P(other-language container shared | English container shared).
(5) Ratification (afterwards, never used to build anything): the share of khora containers that equal Wikidata's own parent (P131) for the English side, for comparison with the link-based reader (9%).
Splits: DEV = F1-F4 (one erratum; then FREEZE); HELD-OUT F5 once (the soft chain of PREREG-SOFTCHAIN with these containers: area first, region breaks ties).
Falsifiers (in advance): (a) reader coverage under 0.80 of leads with a referent in any language: khora's referents are not available there; (b) destroy-set agreement not above the shuffle null's 95th percentile in a language; (c) on F5, fewer than 30 eligible claimants in a language: too small; truth kept < 0.90; or unique-and-correct not above area alone with non-overlapping intervals.
Not claimed: that a neighbour is a container in the khora sense; anything about it/they or noun-phrase referents (not in the reading yet, per the coreference session).
