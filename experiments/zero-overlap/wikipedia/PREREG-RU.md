## ADDENDUM-1 (filed before the Russian pairs were built or any Russian scoring; PREREG-RU sha 0e755048f99dfe96)
Why: the Latin-script failure (RESULT.md) shows the plain overlap counter won because names, dates and numerals are ROOTS: load-bearing facts a rewording cannot move. My tokenizer discarded digits, throwing away the best cross-script roots. A root frame goes first in the ladder.
New rung T_root: probes = numerals of two or more digits (years, counts, measures) found in the English unit; score = how many of them also appear, as numerals, in the other-language unit. A numeral licenses itself (identity), so nothing is learned. Same seeded 50-null, same suitors, same admission rule: T_root, T_all, T_mid, T_mid2 are chosen on dev. Additionally a two-frame ladder ROOT>USAGE is reported as judgeFrames does it (same only if the claimant passes and no suitor does; an undecided root frame opens the usage frame; exhausted = gap), on dev first, held-out only if the admission rule admits it.
Falsifier: if T_root alone, or ROOT>USAGE, does not beat the plain-overlap baseline on balanced accuracy at suitor-pass no higher, then roots as numerals are not what separates the real pair; say so.
Disclosure: numerals are a declared structural class, not tuned; names in Latin letters inside Russian intros are still shared tokens and stay in the baseline.

## ADDENDUM-2 (filed before Russian pairs were built or scored; supersedes the ROOT claim of ADDENDUM-1)
Correction from the owner: a root is common ground, not matching facts. Branches differ, leaves can be sick, facts can be off, yet both texts reach down into the same soil. ADDENDUM-1's numerals are LEAVES; T_root is kept only as a labelled leaf diagnostic, not as the root frame.
Root frame T_ground: G(article) = the set of ground entities its lead section links to, each named by its ENGLISH title (Russian link targets are mapped to English titles through Russian Wikipedia's own interlanguage links; unmapped targets dropped). score(en p, ru q) = |G(p) intersect G(q)|. Nothing is learned, no shared words are needed, no stated fact has to agree. The same seeded 50 length-matched null articles; stop-ground = the 50 most document-frequent ground entities in the induction set, removed (declared, not tuned); suitors = other articles of the same category; plain-overlap baseline as before.
Second measurement, written in advance ("facts could be off"): among TRUE pairs whose numeral sets share nothing (a sick leaf), T_ground should still say same; T_root cannot. Report T_ground TP on that subset against T_root's.
Third: the verdict is split in two. SAME-ROOT (T_ground passes with no suitor passing) is the recognition; AGREE/DISPUTE is a separate leaf question (numerals and the usage frame), reported as a count of same-root pairs whose numerals disagree. The ladder is GROUND > USAGE > gap, via judgeFrames; chosen on dev by the admission rule; held-out run once.
Falsifier: if T_ground does not beat the plain-overlap baseline on LOW/ZERO pairs at suitor-pass no higher, then shared neighbours are not what separates the real pair, and the "common soil" frame does not work as built.
Disclosure: ground ids come from the same Wikipedia interlanguage resource that supplies the gold. The pair's own link is excluded (an article's self-link and its category are dropped), but the resource is shared: a giver (Wikipedia/Wikidata) nominates the soil, usage (overlap beating a null) licenses it. Lead-only links, first fetch on a rate limit.
