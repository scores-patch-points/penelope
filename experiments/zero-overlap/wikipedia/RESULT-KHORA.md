## CORRECTION (2026-11-19, from the coreference session, checked on disk by it)
I wrote that the fra/deu gaps came from my snapshot lacking the janus priors and from denied access. That is wrong. The no_morphology and no_dotted_prior gaps on fra/deu are REAL: on this machine janus/priors holds morphology-eng.json and dotted-eng.json but NO morphology-fra, morphology-deu, dotted-fra or dotted-deu (it does hold punctuation-deu/eng/fra). readWith looks in KHORA_PRIORS_DIR, a janus checkout beside the workspace, ~/Documents/janus/priors, then the vendored native/priors-shipped, then reports a typed gap. So granting access to that folder would not remove those two gaps; the priors would have to be BUILT (the per-language readiness board, eval/competency/LANGUAGE-COMPETENCY-CURRENT.md in khora main, lists what each language lacks). The thin fra/deu reads are therefore khora's current state for those languages, not an artifact of my snapshot. My earlier "next step: get the janus folder" is withdrawn.
