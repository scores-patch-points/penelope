# PREREG-DMD: equivalence as survival of a meaningful falsification attempt (filed before scoring). DEV ONLY (fr, de act 3).
Definition (user): equivalent if the equivalence survives a meaningful attempt, from a perspective and frame, to falsify it; "meaningful" is defined by a DMD, read as "difference that makes a difference" (reading unconfirmed).
Frame D (difference-finder), perspective = a reader who wants the same account in the other language. The falsification attempt looks for MEANINGFUL material on either side with no counterpart on the other.
DMD (declared, nothing tuned): an item is meaningful if it is a numeral (2+ digits) or a 5-char token that is not among the 50 most frequent in the induction set (acts<=2) and whose document frequency in the induction set is at or below the median of its side. An item has a counterpart if the other side contains the same item, or (for a token) a lexicon-licensed partner of it (the induced usage lexicon M, as in the earlier frames), or the same numeral.
Score D(p, q) = (meaningful unmatched on p + on q) / (meaningful items on p + on q); lower is better. PASS (survives) iff D < the minimum D over the claimant's 50 length-matched null candidates (seed 20261118) and D < 1 (default Other).
Arms: natural suitors (first scene-mate); informed adversary with the earlier strategies (copy, usage, num, mix; budgets k=10, 30), once on the first scene-mate and once on the SHORTEST scene-mate (the strongest choice: least own content to leave unmatched). The adversary may add tokens, never delete.
Reported: TP (true pair survives), natural suitor-pass, FA_adv. Compared against ladder A (overlap, usage, numerals) FA_adv from RESULT-ADV (0.4-0.9 at k=10).
Falsifiers (in advance): (1) FA_adv of frame D at k=10, mix, shortest mate > 0.10 in either language: the difference-finder is also forgeable. (2) TP < 0.20: robustness is bought by rejecting the true pairs. Either one means frame D, as built, does not resist forging.
Not tested: adversaries that rewrite sentences or delete content; Russian; a held-out (consumed).
