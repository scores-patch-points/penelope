# PREREG-GROUND-REF: the "common soil" (ground) frame, at the REFERENT level (filed before it was scored)
This is PREREG-RU ADDENDUM-2's ground frame, never run (the data fetch stalled), now done properly with the referent layer: referents, not strings.
Frame T_ground_ref: for a pair (English article p, other-language article q), SE(p) = the set of REFERENTS (Wikidata items, via the resolver of PREREG-L1 ADDENDUM-2) that p's lead links to; SO(q) = the same for q's lead in its own language. score(p, q) = |SE(p) ∩ SO(q)| after removing (a) the pair's own referent (the Q-id of p's page; a self-link) and (b) STOP-GROUND = the 50 referents held by the most leads across F1-F4 (declared, then frozen). String-only referents (no Wikidata item) can never match across languages and are reported as a share. Nothing is learned; no word has to match; no stated fact has to agree.
Pass: score above the maximum score of 50 length-matched null candidates (the other-language leads of other articles, matched on the number of resolved links within max(5, 30%), seed 20261118) and >= 1. Suitors = the other members of the same category. Default Other.
Baseline: plain shared-token overlap (5-character prefixes of lower-cased words of 3+ letters, the 50 most frequent types removed, same null). Strata by baseline overlap: ZERO (0 shared tokens), LOW (<= 1), SOME.
Splits: DEV = F1-F4 (the pairs already read for other frames; one erratum allowed; then FREEZE); HELD-OUT = F5 (fetched unread), run ONCE. Disclosure: F1-F4 were dev data for earlier frames; this frame has not seen them.
Reported (fr and de, each stratum): TP (true pair passes), suitor pass (false accept), BA = (TP + 1 - suitor pass) / 2, for T_ground_ref and the baseline; share of lead links resolved to a Q-id; the share of true pairs with at least one shared referent.
Falsifier (in advance): if T_ground_ref does not beat the baseline's BA on the LOW and ZERO strata at a suitor pass no higher, the shared-soil frame does not separate the true pair even with referents resolved, and "common soil" does not work as built (on F5 the same reading, with n >= 30 in a stratum; a stratum under 30 is "too small").
Not claimed: that a shared referent proves identity (near-twin siblings share the same country and region by construction: the stop-ground and the null are what keep that from passing); non-Latin scripts (the referent layer should make them possible: left for a Russian run).
