# PREREG-TIE: a subject-tie mark on claims (filed before scoring)
Problem (RESULT-REFERENT F2): a stated year is not enough; the number nearest "population" often describes a sub-entity (the capital, a town) named in the same clause. Mark: TIED = no competing named entity near the number; a claim may FALSIFY only if it has a stated time AND is tied on both sides; otherwise a would-be dispute is blocked (a hole).
Rule T1 (declared, nothing learned). For the chosen number m in a lead: window = 40 characters before and after the number. COMPETITOR = a capitalised word (3+ letters) in the window that is not sentence-initial, not part of ALLOWED, not a month name (en, fr, de), and not a source word (Census, Recensement, Zensus, INSEE, Eurostat, ISTAT, Statistik, Statistics, Bureau, Office, Estimate, Estimation, Schätzung, UN, ONU). ALLOWED = the words of the English title plus every word in the first 80 characters of that lead (the subject's own name, whatever the language). UNTIED if a competitor exists, or if a capital marker (capital, capitale, chef-lieu, Hauptstadt, Hauptort, Verwaltungssitz, seat, largest city, plus grande ville, größte Stadt) occurs within 60 characters before the number. Else TIED.
Compared: V0 = extractor R2 as frozen (time rule only); V1 = V0 + the tie gate (stated time AND tied both sides, else a would-be dispute is blocked).
Stage A: F1 + F2 pooled (fr 322, de 297 pairs; these were fresh for R2 but are now the DEV for T1: disclosed, a third use). Read every remaining true-pair dispute; ONE erratum allowed; then FREEZE.
Stage B: a NEW fresh set F3, categories declared before it is fetched, chosen by member count only; run once.
Reported: for V0 and V1 on each set: coverage that can still falsify (stated + tied), true-pair dispute rate with a Wilson 95% interval, suitor dispute rate (stated + tied), share of claims blocked by the gate.
Falsifiers (in advance), on Stage B: (1) V1 true-pair dispute upper end of the 95% interval > 0.10 with n >= 30: the tie does not fix the referent; (2) V1 suitor dispute < 0.80: the number is still too weak an identifier; (3) falsifiable coverage (stated + tied, both sides, as a share of pairs) < 0.03.
Not tested: other fluents; a rewriting adversary; a parser-based tie (this is a window rule).

## ERRATUM-1 (Stage A first run, before any F3 fetch). T1 FAILED on its own dev; replaced by T2, then FROZEN.
Stage A result of T1 (pooled F1+F2): it removed every true-pair dispute (fr 0/50, de 0/82) but destroyed the frame's power: falsifiable coverage 8/322 (fr, 0.025) and 1/297 (de, 0.003), suitor dispute 0.754 (fr) and 0.031 (de). The competitor test (any capitalised non-title word within 40 characters) marks almost every claim untied ("a province of the Netherlands", nationality adjectives), so nothing can falsify. Falsifiers (2) and (3) hit on dev.
T2 (the one allowed revision, written after seeing that): drop the capitalised-word competitor test; a claim is UNTIED only if a ROLE marker for a different kind of entity occurs in its clause, i.e. between the last sentence boundary (. ! ? ;) or 100 characters before the number (whichever is nearer) and the number: capital, capitale, chef-lieu, Hauptstadt, Hauptort, Verwaltungssitz, seat, siège, Sitz, city, ville, Stadt, town, municipality, commune, Gemeinde, largest city, plus grande ville, größte Stadt, metropolitan, agglomération, urban. Everything else unchanged (V0/V1 definitions, gate, stage B, falsifiers). Known gaps by construction: two definitions of the same entity (Delhi city vs the territory) are not detected; a role marker can sit in the clause of the right number.
