# PREREG-TIME: time as a first-class dependency, stated or inferred (filed before scoring). DEV ONLY.
Why dev only: the held-out acts were used once by PREREG-FLUENT (with a disclosed ordering flaw) and earlier frames; a second use for a revision would be reuse. A fresh held-out needs new categories (a declared gap).
Change from PREREG-FLUENT: a population claim with no stated as-of year is no longer BLOCKED. Its time is INFERRED and carried as an uncertain dependency: the interval [2026 - W, 2026], where 2026 is the observation year (all articles were fetched 2026-11-18; no per-article revision date is available) and W = 5 years (DECLARED, not tuned; sensitivity W in {2, 5, 10} reported for transparency). A claim with a stated year keeps the interval [year, year].
Comparison (mirrors organs/frame-verdicts.mjs compareClaims with atInferred): agree if relative difference <= tol (0.05). If either time is inferred: the widest possible gap = max over the two intervals of the distance between endpoints; compatible if the difference <= tol + maxRate (0.03/yr) x widest gap, else DISPUTE (even the widest drift cannot explain it). Stated-both: as before.
Extraction rules: exactly those frozen by PREREG-FLUENT ERRATUM-1 (no change), same pairs, same dev split (acts <= 3).
Reported: true pairs and suitor trials, split into stated-both vs involves-inferred, with agree / compatible / dispute; the blocked count must be 0.
Falsifiers (in advance): (1) true-pair dispute rate among inferred-involved comparisons > 0.10; (2) suitor dispute rate among inferred-involved trials < 0.80 (the carried uncertainty makes the frame too weak to falsify impostors); (3) suitor agree+compatible rate among inferred-involved trials > 0.20 (inferred time manufactures agreement).
Not claimed: coverage (2.6-2.7%) is unchanged by this: it is a referent/extraction limit, not a time limit. No held-out. No other fluents.
